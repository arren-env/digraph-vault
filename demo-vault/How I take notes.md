# How I take notes

I keep atomic notes and link them aggressively, loosely following the [[Zettelkasten]] method.
Each note should be an [[Evergreen notes|evergreen note]]: one idea, written so future me can reuse it.

Daily notes are a scratchpad. Anything worth keeping gets promoted into its own note during the [[Weekly Review Template|weekly review]].
